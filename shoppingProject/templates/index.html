Online Shopper Revenue Prediction

This tool predicts whether an online shopping session is likely to result in revenue based on session-level behavioral data.

📊 Features Used in the Model

Administrative

Number of administrative pages visited (account settings, profile pages, etc.).

Administrative_Duration

Total time spent on administrative sections. Longer duration may indicate confusion or careful account review.

Informational

Number of informational pages visited (FAQs, policy pages, help docs).

Informational_Duration

Total time spent reading informational content, which can indicate purchase intent or uncertainty.

ProductRelated

Number of product-related pages viewed (product detail pages, comparisons).

ProductRelated_Duration

Total time browsing product pages — always strongly correlated with conversion likelihood.

BounceRates

The percentage of times users land on a page and leave immediately. High bounce rates → lower purchase intent.

ExitRates

The percentage of times users exit the site from a specific page. High exit rates often signal friction.

PageValues

An internal measure of how "valuable" each page is empirically based on previous conversions.

SpecialDay

A number between 0–1 showing how close the session is to a special event like Valentine's Day or Mother's Day.

Month

The month of the session. Seasonality strongly affects purchase likelihood.

VisitorType

Whether the user is new, returning, or other. Returning visitors are much more likely to convert.

Weekend

Indicates if the session happened on a weekend. Behavior patterns differ by day of week.
